# 🐈‍⬛ Chomusuke

Desktop music player that streams audio straight from torrents 🎶

## ✨ Features

- 🧲 **Play from a torrent** — paste a magnet link, info hash or `.torrent` link, or open a `.torrent` file. It shows up as
  an album with tracks, artist and cover.
- ▶️ **Instant playback** — music starts within seconds, without waiting for the download. Seek anywhere, queue an
  album, and use your media keys.
- 🔍 **Search** — one search across all your music sources. Click an artist's name anywhere to see everything by them.
- 🏠 **Home** — more from the artists you play most, plus a chart in their genre. Your listening history stays on your
  computer and can be cleared in Settings.
- 🧭 **Explore** — top albums and songs from Spotify, YouTube Music, Apple Music and ListenBrainz, by country or language.
  Pick one and Chomusuke finds it and plays it.
- 🔌 **Sources and plugins** — add the music sources you like in Settings. The Internet Archive and several free-music
  collections are ready to use, popular torrent sites can be switched on, and more can be installed as plugins.
- 💾 **Save offline** — keep whole albums so they play instantly, even without a connection. Recently played music is
  kept too, within a disk limit you choose.
- 👍 **Likes and dislikes** — rate songs, and disliked songs are skipped. Your ratings follow a song across different
  releases.
- 🎼 **Playlists** — mix songs from any album and any source, reorder, shuffle, and move on to the next album
  automatically.
- 🤝 **Sharing** — choose whether Chomusuke shares what you have with other listeners, and set an upload limit. One switch
  turns all sharing off.
- 💬 **Discord status** — show what you're listening to, with a button that sends friends to Chomusuke. Optional.
- 🎨 **Make it yours** — ready-made light and dark themes, or pick your own colours, backgrounds, fonts and effects, even
  drifting sakura petals or snow.
- 📦 **Backup** — move your ratings, saved albums, playlists and look to another computer.
- 🔄 **Updates** — Chomusuke tells you when a new version is out.

## ⬇️ Download

Get the latest build from **[Releases → Latest](https://github.com/notKvS/chomusuke/releases/latest)** (under
*Assets*):

| System | File |
|---|---|
| 🪟 Windows | `Chomusuke-<version>-win-x64.exe` (installer) or `-win-x64.zip` (portable) |
| 🍎 macOS (Apple silicon) | `Chomusuke-<version>-mac-arm64.dmg` |
| 🐧 Linux | `Chomusuke-<version>-linux-x86_64.AppImage` or `-linux-amd64.deb` |

Builds are unsigned: on Windows choose *More info → Run anyway*; on macOS right-click the app → *Open* the first
time.
